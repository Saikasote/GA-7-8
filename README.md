## 📑 Dataset

| Quarter | Turnover |
| ------- | -------- |

| Q4      | 10.70    |
| Average | 5.19     |

---

## 🔎 Key Findings

- Turnover increased steadily from **Q1 (1.94)** to **Q4 (10.70)**.
- The **average turnover = 5.19**, below the **target of 8**.
- Strong Q4 performance indicates progress, but consistency is lacking.

---

## 📈 Business Implications

- **Low turnover** → Inventory is moving slowly → Higher holding costs.
- **Capital is locked** → Less liquidity for growth.
- **Volatile quarterly performance** → Demand planning issues.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

To achieve and sustain turnover ≥ 8:

1. **Optimize supply chain** → reduce lead times and improve supplier reliability.
2. **Enhance demand forecasting** → use data-driven models to align production with demand.
3. **Balance stock levels** → avoid overstocking while ensuring availability.
